# Notice

automotive-hmi-ux is an independent community project. It is not made, endorsed or reviewed by any car maker, regulator, rating body or standards body it mentions. It began as the automotive skill in [criticalUX](https://github.com/Sthitapragna-009/Critical_UX) by the same author.

- **Not legal, homologation, functional-safety or engineering advice.** The skill summarises public regulations, rating protocols, platform guidelines and research as design guidance. Rules change; read the linked primary source and check with your homologation, functional-safety (ISO 26262 / SOTIF) and human-factors teams before relying on any rule for a production vehicle.
- **Paraphrased, not copied.** Regulations, protocols and articles are summarised in original words with links. Short quotations are limited to a few words where the wording matters.
- **Paywalled standards were not reproduced.** ISO 15005, 15008, 16951 and 2575, SAE J2364 and J3016, and JAMA guidelines are referred to only where public summaries exist, and the skill marks those rules "not verified".
- **Confidence tags.** Every rule is tagged by source type: regulation, standard or platform guideline, published research, documented company practice, or our own inference. Inferences are our reasoning, not a source's claim.
- **Trademarks.** Company, product and programme names (for example Tesla, Autopilot, Volkswagen, BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Hyundai, Polestar, Volvo, Apple, CarPlay, Google, Android Auto, Android Automotive, Royal Enfield, Ather, Euro NCAP, IIHS, NHTSA, Airbus) are trademarks of their owners, used only to name what the guidance describes.
- **No brand assets.** The repo ships no logos, proprietary fonts, tell-tale artwork, screenshots of vehicles or apps, or brand colour palettes.
