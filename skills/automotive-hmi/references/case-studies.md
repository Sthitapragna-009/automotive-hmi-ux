# Automotive case studies

What OEMs and platforms did, and the rule worth taking from it. **FACT** = documented; **COMMENTARY** = press or analyst opinion.

| Product | What happened | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Tesla Model 3/Y** | Removed the indicator stalks in the 2023 Model 3 refresh; reinstated the stalk as standard in China in Aug 2025 after "nearly two years of customer feedback", with a ¥2,499 retrofit (FACT) ([CarScoops](https://www.carscoops.com/2025/08/tesla-model-3-china-turn-signal-stalk-return/)). Autopilot recall 23V-838 for insufficient control prominence (FACT, see automation). | Basic controls stay physical; automation needs prominent, simple mode controls |
| **Volkswagen ID family** | Touch sliders drew criticism; CEO Schäfer called physical buttons "non-negotiable" (Mar 2026); VW runs customer clinics and in-car cameras to see what drivers use (FACT) ([CarScoops](https://www.carscoops.com/2026/03/volkswagen-interior-controls-reset/)) | Observe real drivers, then decide what is physical |
| **Hyundai / Kia** | Design head Sang Yup Lee committed (2023) to keep hard keys "for as long as possible" because they are easy to sense and feel (FACT) ([Motor1](https://www.motor1.com/news/658027/hyundai-pledges-keep-physical-buttons/)) | Feel-findable controls are a feature |
| **Mercedes MBUX Hyperscreen** | >141 cm glass; "zero layer" with no menu levels for key apps; 20+ functions suggested by context; 12 haptic actuators; new LLM assistant with Google (FACT) ([Mercedes-Benz Group](https://group.mercedes-benz.com/technologie/digitalisierung/konnektivitaet/mbux-hyperscreen.html)) | Reduce depth; one-tap accept/dismiss for suggestions |
| **BMW Panoramic iDrive (Neue Klasse, iX3)** | "Eyes on the road, hands on the wheel"; pillar-to-pillar projection at the windscreen base; screen angled to the driver's arm; haptic wheel pads with raised elements; rotary controller dropped because its use declined year by year (FACT) ([Auto Express](https://autoexpress.co.uk/bmw/365522/new-bmw-panoramic-idrive-revealed-latest-iteration-tech-changed-motoring)) | Place information near the line of sight; base decisions on usage data |
| **Polestar (Android Automotive)** | Single typeface, restricted palette, content on demand, mirrored cluster and centre screens (FACT, design lead interview) ([GoAuto](https://goauto.com.au/amp/93827/polestar-details-hi-tech-hmi-for-3-and-4-suvs.html)) | Restraint is the design system |
| **Apple CarPlay Ultra** | Launched 15 May 2025 with Aston Martin; covers the instrument cluster with gauge themes co-designed with each OEM; works with physical buttons for climate and settings (FACT) ([Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2025/05/carplay-ultra-the-next-generation-of-carplay-begins-rolling-out-today/)) | Phone platforms are taking the cluster; physical controls stay |
| **Xiaomi SU7** | Optional magnetic docking keys under the centre screen (FACT, press) | Buyers want physical controls back, even as accessories |
| **Volvo V70 (2005)** | Fastest in the Vi Bilägare test at 10.0 s for four tasks (FACT) | The baseline every new design should beat |
| **Royal Enfield Tripper Dash** | Single joystick, glance-first Analog mode, auto day/night, survives phone-off and signal loss (FACT) | Minimal by default, more on request |
| **AtherStack 7** | Voice, two-wheeler routing, fall and theft safety, OTA (FACT) | Safety features are part of the dashboard |

Not researched: Rivian, Lucid, Porsche, Toyota, Scout, NIO, Li Auto, BYD, Ola, BMW Motorrad, Ducati.
